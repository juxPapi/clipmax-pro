# Clipmax Pro — Video Tutorial Script (for Nano Banana)

Use this scene-by-scene script to generate a tutorial video. Each scene has a **visual prompt** (feed to Nano Banana) and **narration/on-screen text**. Suggested length: ~2 minutes. Aspect ratio: 16:9. Style: dark maroon cinematic, clean, no emoji, condensed display type (Anton), Inter body, gold `#F2C14E` accents.

---

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual prompt:** "Cinematic 16:9 studio desk, deep maroon and black gradient background, a single monitor glowing with a modern video-editing interface, gold light streaks, film grain, no people, no text, moody dramatic lighting."

**On-screen text:** `Find the moment.`
**Narration:** "Long streams hide great clips. Clipmax Pro finds them, cuts them, captions them, and queues them for every platform — all on your own machine."

---

## Scene 2 — What it is (0:10–0:25)
**Visual prompt:** "Clean dark app UI mockup, left sidebar with 'Library / Queue / Settings', main area showing a grid of source video cards with thumbnails and scores, deep maroon panels, gold accent badges, professional SaaS look, no real brand logos."

**On-screen text:** `Local-first clipper studio`
**Narration:** "Everything runs locally. Paste a stream link or drop a recording. The file never leaves your computer until you choose to publish."

---

## Scene 3 — Add a source (0:25–0:40)
**Visual prompt:** "Close-up of a link input field with placeholder 'https://twitch.tv/streamer/clip/...' and a gold 'Pull the stream' button, next to a dashed drop zone reading 'Drop a recording here', dark UI, film grain."

**On-screen text:** `01 Link or file`
**Narration:** "Start in the Library. Paste a Twitch, YouTube, Kick or X link — or drag in an MP4, MOV or MKV file. yt-dlp pulls the best video it can find."

---

## Scene 4 — The listen (0:40–0:55)
**Visual prompt:** "Abstract visualization of audio waveform turning into text captions, glowing gold waveform bars on dark maroon, a small whisper icon, clean data-viz style, no words."

**On-screen text:** `02 Listen locally`
**Narration:** "FFmpeg extracts the audio and a local Whisper model transcribes every word. First run downloads the model once; after that it's offline. No cloud, no per-minute fees."

---

## Scene 5 — The heatmap (0:55–1:10)
**Visual prompt:** "Signature interest-curve heatmap: gold and ember vertical bars across a timeline, two highlighted cut-window rectangles, a playhead line, dark background, dense data visualization, elegant."

**On-screen text:** `03 Score the peaks`
**Narration:** "Every candidate window is scored on hooks, questions, numbers, emotion, pace and audio energy. The heatmap shows exactly where the room spikes — and the cut list explains why."

---

## Scene 6 — Cut & caption (1:10–1:25)
**Visual prompt:** "Vertical video preview in a 9:16 frame with animated captions burned in, controls around it: aspect 9:16 / 4:5 / 1:1, layout Fill / Blur / Split / Triple, a grid of colorful caption style swatches, dark studio UI."

**On-screen text:** `04 Cut vertical`
**Narration:** "Reframe to 9:16, 4:5 or 1:1. Pan, zoom, trim. Pick from 56 caption styles. The preview matches the final render — what you see is what exports."

---

## Scene 7 — Render & export (1:25–1:40)
**Visual prompt:** "Progress bar filling during a render, then a list of finished MP4 exports with duration, size and style labels, gold success accents, dark UI."

**On-screen text:** `Render 1080×1920 · H.264 + AAC · −16 LUFS`
**Narration:** "Hit render. FFmpeg burns in the captions, normalizes loudness, and writes a polished vertical MP4. Every export is listed and replayable."

---

## Scene 8 — Publish (1:40–2:00)
**Visual prompt:** "A publishing queue with four platform rows — YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Page — each with its own status dot, a 'Dry run' badge at the top, dark UI, gold and green accents."

**On-screen text:** `Post everywhere. Dry run first.`
**Narration:** "Queue one cut for all four platforms with real official APIs. Dry run is on by default, so you can test the whole flow safely. When ready, connect your accounts in Settings, turn dry run off, and post for real."

**On-screen text:**

## Production notes for Nano Banana
- Keep typography condensed and uppercase for headings (Anton-like), body in a clean sans (Inter-like).
- Palette: background `#080405` → `#14070d`, panels `#1c0a12`, text bone `#f5eee3`, accent gold `#f2c14e` and ember `#e4572e`.
- Add subtle film grain (5% opacity) over every scene; no emoji, no purple gradients.
- Transition between scenes with a short crossfade (~0.4s) and a soft gold light sweep.
- If the tool supports it, reuse a consistent "host" avatar or keep it purely UI-driven for coherence.
- Narration voice: calm, confident, medium pace, low register.
