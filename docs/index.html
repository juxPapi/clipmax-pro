Clipmax Pro

A local-first AI clip studio. Paste a stream link or drop a recording — Clipmax Pro transcribes it with Whisper, scores every candidate moment with audio + speech signals, then reframes, captions and renders vertical clips ready for every feed. Everything runs on your machine.
